# Peer contribution survey

Complete this short survey on Canvas within **48 hours** after each session in which you presented or critiqued. It takes about two minutes. Responses are confidential and seen only by the instructor.

For **each teammate** (not yourself), rate from 1 (rarely) to 5 (always):

| Item | Statement |
|---|---|
| Preparation | Came prepared and had read the paper closely |
| Share of the work | Did a fair share of the slides, analysis, and rehearsal |
| Reliability | Met internal deadlines and communicated with the group |

Optional: one sentence on anything the instructor should know about how the group worked.

## How it is used

- By default, every member receives the group grade.
- If a member's average rating from teammates is below 3, or comments describe a clear problem, the instructor reviews the case and may lower that member's grade by up to 10%.
- A member who clearly carried extra weight may receive up to 10% more, capped at 100.
- Completing the survey counts toward participation.
