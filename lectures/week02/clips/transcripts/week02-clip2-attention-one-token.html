Attention for one token

Week 2, clip 2 of 5. This is the narration of the clip, which an AI-generated voice reads in the narrated version; the silent version shows the same steps with on-screen captions only.

Let's follow the token cold. Its query vector, in red, is compared with the key vector of every token in the sentence, in green.

Each score is the query for cold dotted with a key, then divided by the square root of d. The scaled scores are minus 0.5, 0.2, 1.3, 0.6, and 1.6.

The highest scores go to cold itself, at 1.6, and to has, at 1.3. These are the tokens that matter most here.

A softmax turns the five scores into attention weights. They are all between zero and one, and together they sum to one.

The new vector for cold is the weighted sum of the five value vectors, in blue. The values of has and cold contribute the most.

Now the vector for cold carries its context. In the phrase has a cold, the model can represent cold as an infection, not a temperature.
